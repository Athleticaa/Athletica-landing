1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Athletica, we may collect the following information:

When you create or use an Athletica account, we may collect:

  • Username
  • Email address
  • Password or authentication credentials
  • Account role, such as Coach or Client

If you use Google Sign-In, we may receive information provided by Google that is necessary to authenticate your account, such as your email address, name, profile information, and authentication identifiers.

Profile Information

You may provide information for your Athletica profile, including:

  • Profile information
  • Profile image

  • Coach or client information
  • Information required to complete your profile

Messages and Communication

Athletica provides communication features between coaches and clients. We may collect and process:

  • Text messages
  • Images sent through chat
  • Voice messages
  • Message attachments
  • Related conversation and delivery information

Coach Certificates

Coaches may upload professional certificates or other supporting documents through the application. These documents may include PDF files and information associated with the uploaded certificate, such as its title.

Technical and Diagnostic Information

We use Sentry to help monitor application errors, crashes, and technical issues.

Sentry may collect technical information associated with application errors, which can include:

  • Device and operating system information
  • Application version
  • Error and crash information
  • Diagnostic information
  • Relevant technical context associated with an error

We use this information to identify, diagnose, and fix technical problems and improve application reliability.
